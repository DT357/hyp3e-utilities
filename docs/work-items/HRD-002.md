# HRD-002 — Authorization and Payload-Validation Audit

## Implemented outcome

Reviewed all 21 player-triggerable authoritative Party Sheet operations and
the client-only HUD, Party action, treasury lifecycle, and marching-report
surfaces. The reviewed operation matrix and trust boundary are recorded in
`docs/security-and-validation-audit.md`.

The audit corrected two defects: idempotency keys are now scoped to requester,
operation, and request ID; and every authoritative operation independently
fails closed unless its executing client is Foundry's active GM.

## Manual checks

This work item audits and hardens the shared protocol already exercised in the
Foundry 13.351 / `hyp3e` 4.0.3 and Foundry 14.365 / `hyp3e` 4.1.0 Milestone 5
multi-user distribution gate. The final clean-world compatibility matrix is
tracked by HRD-005.

## Known limitations and follow-up

  party authority. This is the approved permission model, not an Actor-by-Actor
  collaboration system.
- HRD-005 repeats the hardened protocol paths in clean Foundry 13 and 14 roots.

No design-document change was required.
